Every legendary silicon architect — from the pioneers of Bell Labs and Fairchild Semiconductor to modern processor designers — began at the exact same coordinate: zero experience. He has just started his learning journey at JECRC University, Jaipur after graduating from SBS Convent School, and he carries an unshakeable willingness to go far away in this field.

Whether it is semiconductor doping, CMOS fabrication, or bare-metal microcontroller firmware, this gazette documents the climb from the very first step.
